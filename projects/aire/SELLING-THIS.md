# How To Sell J9-AiRE (For Karl)

You don't need to know how the engine works. You need to know what the car does
and why someone would pay for it.

---

## The Pitch (30 seconds)

"You have 200 clients. You can't personally stay in touch with all of them. When
you go quiet for 60 days, they forget you exist — and the next time they sell,
they call someone else. J9-AiRE makes sure that never happens. It tracks every
relationship, tells you who to call today, and drafts messages in your voice so
your clients never feel neglected."

---

## The Problem You're Solving

Top luxury agents have a specific pain point: they're TOO successful. They have
hundreds of past clients who generated millions in commissions — but they can't
stay in touch with all of them. So relationships decay, referrals dry up, and
repeat business goes to the agent who happened to call last week.

Hiring assistants doesn't fix it. Janine's exact words: "My two assistants
basically do nothing."

CRMs don't fix it either. They're spreadsheets with a skin. They track data but
don't DO anything. The agent still has to remember who needs what, write every
message, and make every call.

---

## What J9-AiRE Does That Nothing Else Does

1. **It thinks for you.** It doesn't just store data — it tells you "Call Phil
   Knight's property manager, you haven't talked to them since April."

2. **It writes like you.** Not generic templates. Messages drafted in YOUR voice,
   based on YOUR writing style, referencing specific details only YOU would know.

3. **It never forgets.** Spouse name, kids, dog's name, favorite restaurant,
   property preferences — it remembers everything so you don't have to.

4. **It protects the relationship.** When a client goes 60 days without hearing
   from you, the system flags it before the relationship goes cold.

## The Demo Flow (When Showing Someone)

1. Open the app on your phone (it's designed for mobile)
2. Show the client list — "These are all your clients, sorted by who needs attention"
3. Tap a client — "Everything about this person in one place. Birthday, spouse,
   last time you talked, every deal you've done together"
4. Show the follow-up banner — "The app tells you who to call TODAY"
5. Tap Log Interaction — "After a call, tap this, write a one-line summary. Done.
   The system updates everything automatically"
6. Import — "Got a spreadsheet? Upload it. Works with Google Contacts, iPhone,
   any CRM export. Takes 30 seconds"
