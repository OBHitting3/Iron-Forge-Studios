## 3. The Equity: What You Get

### Recommended Structure: 75/25 Split

Based on industry standards for this exact situation (solo technical founder bringing on a business-side COO after initial build):

| Factor | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| I originated the idea | Founder premium |
| I built all 8 projects before you join | Sweat equity already invested |
| All existing code, architecture, research is mine | IP contribution |
| You're joining pre-revenue but post-MVP | Less risk than Day 1, but still high risk |
| You bring business/numbers skills I lack | Critical gap filled |
| No salary for either of us initially | Equal sacrifice going forward |

### The Numbers

| | OBHitting3 (CEO) | Jake (COO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Equity** | **75%** | **25%** |
| Vesting | 4 years, 1-year cliff | 4 years, 1-year cliff |
| At 12-month cliff | 75% fully accessible | 6.25% vested (25% × 25%) |
| Monthly after cliff | — | +0.52%/month |
| Fully vested (48 months) | 75% | 25% |

### Why 75/25 and Not 50/50

| Scenario | Typical Split | Why |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| Both start from scratch together, Day 1 | 50/50 or 55/45 | Equal risk, equal contribution |
| Technical founder + business cofounder joining later | 60/40 to 70/30 | IP and sweat equity premium |
| **Solo founder with shipped MVPs + COO joining pre-revenue** | **70/30 to 80/20** | **Significant IP already created** |
| Founder hiring COO employee post-revenue | 2–5% (options) | Reduced risk, salary paid |

**Industry benchmarks say 70/30 to 80/20 is standard for this situation.** I'm recommending 75/25 because:
1. I've already built the products (months of work)
2. But you're taking real risk joining pre-revenue with no salary
3. 25% of a successful portfolio is substantial money
4. It's enough to keep you motivated for years
5. It's fair to both of us

### The Cliff Protects Both of Us

The 1-year cliff means:
- If you leave before 12 months → you keep 0%
- If I leave before 12 months → I lose 75% of my vesting (keep what's vested)
- After 12 months, equity vests monthly — you earn it as you go
- This is **standard for every startup in the world** — it protects the company and the friendship

## 6. What We Put in Writing

### Non-Negotiable Protections (For Both of Us)

| Protection | What It Does |
|------------|-------------|
| **4-year vesting, 1-year cliff** | Neither of us walks away with equity we didn't earn |
| **Founder agreement** | Defines roles, equity, decision-making, dispute resolution |
| **IP assignment** | All existing code is assigned to the company (not personal) |
| **Decision-making** | CEO has final say on product/technical; COO has final say on operations/business; major decisions (>$5K spend, new hires, equity changes) require both |
| **Exit provisions** | What happens if one of us wants out — buyback terms, right of first refusal |
| **Non-compete** | Neither of us builds competing products while active |
| **Dissolution clause** | If we shut down, how assets and IP are divided |

### What You Should Ask a Lawyer About

- LLC vs. S-Corp (tax implications at our revenue level)
- Operating agreement template
- Vesting schedule documentation
- IP assignment for existing code
- Whether to do a formal valuation before equity grant
